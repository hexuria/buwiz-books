## Overview

**Departments** allow you to tag transactions with organizational divisions, enabling you to track revenue and expenses by department.

<img src="/images/dimensions/departments.png" alt="Departments" />

## Managing Departments

### Creating a Department

1. Navigate to **Departments** in the Accounting section of the sidebar
2. Click **+ New Department**
3. Enter the department name (e.g., "Engineering", "Sales", "Marketing")
4. Add an optional description and manager

### Department List

The Departments page displays all departments with:

- **Department Name**
- **Description**
- **Transaction Count** — Number of tagged transactions
- **Total Amount** — Sum of all transactions tagged to this department

## Tagging Transactions

When creating or editing a transaction, select a **Department** from the dropdown to tag it. This enables:

- **Departmental P&L** — See revenue and expenses per department
- **Budget tracking** — Compare actual spend against departmental budgets
- **Cost allocation** — Understand which departments drive costs

## Department Insights

Click any department to see its detail page:

- **Transaction list** — All entries tagged to this department
- **Spending breakdown** — By account category
- **Timeline** — Monthly spend trends

<Tip>
  Departments are especially useful for businesses with multiple teams or profit centers. Tag
  transactions consistently for the most accurate reporting.
</Tip>
