How to match a Transaction Manually?

Go to the Ledger: From the left-hand menu, select Accounting → Ledger.
Enter Edit Mode: Click the Edit icon at the top of the Ledger to enable editing.
Find Your Transactions: Use filters or scroll to locate the two transactions you want to match (for example, a payment and a corresponding deposit for the same amount).
Select Transactions: Click the checkbox next to each of the two transactions you want to match.
Match: A toolbar will appear at the top. Click the Match icon.
Confirm: Confirm the match when prompted. The matched transactions will merge into a single, clean record in your Ledger.
Example: Let's say you have a $500 payment from your bank and a $500 deposit in Stripe. Find both, check them, and click Match. They will merge into one entry.

Requirements:

The amounts must be identical.
Other info (date, party, category) helps, but isn't required.

What happens next?

The transactions are consolidated into one record.
Reports will only count the transaction once.
Changes may take a few minutes to show in the Ledger and Reconciliation views.

What kind of transaction can be matched?
You can match any transactions that share the same total amount, including payments, deposits, or transfers between connected accounts.

What happens when  transactions are matched?
Buwiz Books merges the two entries into one consolidated record, removing duplicates and syncing the data across your Ledger and Reconciliation views.

Do matches affects reports?
Yes — matched transactions are counted once across financial reports, ensuring your totals remain accurate.

✨ Buwiz Books automatically matches transactions across connected sources—like Stripe, Ramp, or your client’s bank—to merge them into a single, clean record. This prevents duplicates and keeps your books audit-ready.

Most matches happen automatically, but during reconciliation, you can manually match or unmatch transactions if something looks off.
