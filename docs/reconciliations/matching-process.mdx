## The Matching Interface

When you open an active reconciliation, you'll see a split-panel view:

| Panel                     | Contents                                                                  |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Left: Statement Items** | Lines from your bank statement (imported or manually entered)             |
| **Right: Ledger Items**   | Transactions from your Buwiz Books ledger for the same period and account |

## Matching Methods

### Auto-Match

Buwiz Books automatically matches transactions based on:

- **Exact amount match** — Same dollar amount on the same date
- **Near-date match** — Same amount within ±3 days
- **Description similarity** — Fuzzy matching on memo/description text

Auto-matched items appear with a green checkmark. Review them to confirm accuracy.

### Manual Match

For items that weren't auto-matched:

1. Select a statement line item (left panel)
2. Find the corresponding ledger entry (right panel)
3. Click to link them together
4. The pair is marked as matched

### Creating Missing Entries

If a statement line has no matching ledger entry:

1. Select the unmatched statement item
2. Click **Create Transaction**
3. A pre-filled transaction form opens with the date, amount, and description from the statement
4. Assign the correct account category
5. Save — the new entry is automatically matched

## The Difference Indicator

At the top of the reconciliation view, you'll see:

```
Statement Balance:  $45,608.25
Matched Balance:    $44,308.25
Difference:         $1,300.00
```

Your goal is to get the **difference to $0.00**. When it reaches zero, you can complete the reconciliation.

## Completing the Reconciliation

Once all items are matched and the difference is zero:

1. Click **Complete Reconciliation**
2. The session is locked and marked as completed
3. Matched transactions are flagged as reconciled in the ledger

<Warning>
  Completed reconciliations cannot be re-opened. If you discover an error later, create a new
  reconciliation session for the affected period.
</Warning>

## Tips for Efficient Reconciliation

<Tip>
  Reconcile monthly, shortly after receiving your bank statement. The fewer transactions to match,
  the faster the process.
</Tip>

- **Start with auto-matches** — Review and confirm all automatically matched items first
- **Sort by amount** — Use sorting to quickly find matching amounts
- **Check for duplicates** — If the difference is exactly double a transaction amount, you may have a duplicate entry
- **Bank fees and interest** — These are commonly unrecorded. Create entries for them during reconciliation
