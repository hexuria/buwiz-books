## Starting a New Reconciliation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Reconciliations">
    Click **Reconciliations** in the sidebar under Accounting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Reconciliation">Click the **+ New Reconciliation** button.</Step>
  <Step title="Select Bank Account">
    Choose the bank or credit card account you want to reconcile from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Statement Period">
    Enter the **statement start date** and **statement end date** from your bank statement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Statement Balance">
    Input the **ending balance** shown on your bank statement. This is the target balance you're
    reconciling to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Statement (Optional)">
    You can optionally upload a bank statement PDF or CSV. If uploaded, Buwiz Books will use AI to
    extract transactions for automatic matching.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Reconciliation">Click **Create** to begin the matching process.</Step>
</Steps>

## What You'll Need

Before starting, have your bank statement ready with:

| Information          | Where to Find It                                      |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Statement period** | Top of your bank statement                            |
| **Ending balance**   | Bottom of your statement or summary section           |
| **Transaction list** | The detailed line items (if importing for auto-match) |

<Tip>
  Download your bank statement as a CSV from your bank's online portal for the best auto-matching
  results. PDF statements also work via AI OCR extraction.
</Tip>

## Choosing the Right Account

The account dropdown shows all bank-connected accounts in your Chart of Accounts. If you don't see the account you need:

1. Go to **Category Manager**
2. Create a new account under **Assets → Bank Accounts**
3. Return to Reconciliations and it will appear in the dropdown
