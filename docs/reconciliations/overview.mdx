## What is Reconciliation?

**Bank reconciliation** is the process of comparing your internal ledger records against external bank statements to ensure they match. This catches errors, missing transactions, and unauthorized activity.

<img
  src="/images/reconciliations/overview.png"
  alt="Reconciliations dashboard showing accounts grouped by Assets and Liabilities with reconciliation progress"
/>

## Why Reconcile?

- **Catch errors** — Identify data entry mistakes or duplicate entries
- **Find missing transactions** — Discover bank fees, interest, or charges not yet recorded
- **Detect fraud** — Spot unauthorized transactions early
- **Audit readiness** — Maintain clean, verified books

## Reconciliation Workflow

The typical reconciliation process in Buwiz Books:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Reconciliation Session">
    Select the bank account and statement period to reconcile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Statement Balance">Input the ending balance from your bank statement.</Step>
  <Step title="Match Transactions">
    Compare ledger entries against statement lines. Buwiz Books auto-matches where possible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve Discrepancies">
    For unmatched items, either create missing entries or investigate the difference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Reconciliation">
    Once the difference is $0.00, mark the reconciliation as complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reconciliation Status

| Status          | Meaning                                                  |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **In Progress** | Active reconciliation session — still matching           |
| **Completed**   | Fully reconciled — all items matched, difference is zero |
| **Abandoned**   | Session was cancelled without completing                 |
