## What are Connections?

The **Connections Hub** is where you link external services to your organization. It has two purposes:

1. **Payment Gateways** — Accept online payments from customers (Stripe, PayPal)
2. **Bank Accounts & Cards** — Display your company-owned bank details on invoice payment links for manual wire/bank transfers

Navigate to Connections at:

```
Organization → Connections
```

Or directly: `https://your-domain.com/organization/{orgId}/connections`

---

## Tabs

| Tab                  | Purpose                                                        |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Payment Gateways** | Configure Stripe and PayPal for online payments                |
| **Banks & Cards**    | Show company bank accounts on payment links for wire transfers |

---

## How it connects to Invoices

When a customer opens an invoice payment link:

- If **Stripe** is connected → customer sees a "Pay with Card" button
- If **PayPal** is connected → customer sees a "Pay with PayPal" button
- If **company bank accounts** are marked as visible → customer sees wire transfer instructions

<Note>
  A bank account only appears in Connections if it is marked as **Company-owned** in Entities →
  Banks & Cards. Third-party accounts (e.g. vendor bank accounts you've recorded) are intentionally
  excluded.
</Note>
