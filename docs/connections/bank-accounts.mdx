## Overview

The **Banks & Cards** tab in Connections shows your company-owned bank accounts that customers can use for manual wire / bank transfer payments — an alternative to Stripe or PayPal.

---

## Marking an Account as Company-Owned

Only accounts you own appear here. To add an account:

1. Go to **Entities → Banks & Cards**
2. Open or create a bank account
3. Toggle **"Company-owned account"** on
4. Save — the account now appears in **Connections → Banks & Cards**

<Note>
  Third-party accounts (vendor banks, lender accounts, etc.) are intentionally excluded even if
  entered in Entities. Only toggle "Company-owned" for accounts that belong to your business.
</Note>

---

## What information to include

When setting up a bank account for wire transfers, make sure to fill in:

| Field                  | Example                                       |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Account Name           | Goldcoders Corp — Operating                   |
| Institution            | Mercury                                       |
| Account Type           | Checking                                      |
| Account Number         | Full number (not just last 4)                 |
| Routing Number / SWIFT | For domestic / international wires            |
| Ledger Category        | Link to the correct asset account in your COA |

These details are shown to customers on the payment link so they know where to send funds.

---

## Customer experience

When a customer opens a payment link and your org has company-owned bank accounts enabled:

1. They see a **"Bank Transfer"** section with your account details
2. They initiate the transfer from their bank
3. Once you confirm receipt, manually mark the invoice as paid:
   - Open the invoice → **Actions → Record Payment**
   - Select the bank account funds were deposited into
   - Enter the amount received

This creates the journal entry:

| Line | Account             | Debit  | Credit |
| ---- | ------------------- | ------ | ------ |
| 1    | Your Bank Account   | Amount | —      |
| 2    | Accounts Receivable | —      | Amount |
