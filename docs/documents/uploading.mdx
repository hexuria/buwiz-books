## Upload Methods

### Drag and Drop

Drag files directly onto the Documents page to upload them. Multiple files can be uploaded simultaneously.

### Upload Button

Click **+ Upload** and select files from your computer.

### From Other Modules

Documents can also be attached from within:

- **Transaction detail** — Click "Attach Document"
- **Bill creation** — Upload the vendor invoice
- **Invoice creation** — Attach supporting files

## Supported Formats

| Format        | Max Size | Notes                            |
| ------------- | -------- | -------------------------------- |
| **PDF**       | 25 MB    | Best for invoices and statements |
| **JPG / PNG** | 15 MB    | Photos of receipts               |
| **HEIC**      | 15 MB    | iPhone photos (auto-converted)   |

## What Happens After Upload

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload">File is uploaded and stored securely.</Step>
  <Step title="AI Processing">
    The document is analyzed by AI to: - Detect the document type (receipt, invoice, statement,
    etc.) - Extract key data (dates, amounts, vendor names) - Suggest transaction links
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ready for Use">
    The document appears in your library with extracted metadata and is ready to be linked to
    transactions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Organization

Documents are organized by:

- **Date** — Sort by upload date or document date
- **Type** — Filter by classification
- **Status** — Filter by linked/unlinked
- **Search** — Full-text search across document content

<Tip>
  Upload documents as soon as you receive them. The sooner they're in the system, the easier it is
  to match them to transactions during reconciliation.
</Tip>
