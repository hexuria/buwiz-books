## Sending an Invoice

After creating and reviewing an invoice:

1. From the invoice review screen, click **Send Invoice**
2. The customer receives an email with:
   - A professional PDF attachment of the invoice
   - A link to the **Payment Portal** where they can view and pay online
3. The invoice status changes to **Sent**

## Delivery Tracking

Buwiz Books tracks invoice delivery milestones:

| Event      | Description                                               |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sent**   | Email was dispatched to the customer                      |
| **Viewed** | Customer opened the invoice (via payment portal tracking) |
| **Paid**   | Customer completed payment                                |

## Resending an Invoice

If a customer didn't receive the email:

1. Open the invoice
2. Click **⋯ menu → Resend**
3. The email is sent again to the customer's email on file

## Email Customization

The invoice email includes:

- Your organization name and branding
- Invoice number and amount due
- Due date
- A "View & Pay" button linking to the payment portal

<Note>
  Email delivery depends on your email infrastructure configuration. Ensure your organization
  settings have valid sender details configured.
</Note>

## Following Up on Unpaid Invoices

For invoices that are past due:

1. Open the overdue invoice
2. Click **Send Reminder** to send a follow-up email
3. The reminder includes the original invoice details with a "Past Due" notification

<Tip>
  Use the [AR Aging Report](/financials/aging-reports) to see all outstanding customer balances
  organized by how overdue they are.
</Tip>
