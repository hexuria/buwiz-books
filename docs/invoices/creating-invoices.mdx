## Creating a New Invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click + New Invoice">From the Invoices page, click **+ New Invoice**.</Step>
  <Step title="Select Customer">
    Choose an existing customer or create a new one. Customer details (name, email, address) are
    auto-populated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Invoice Details">
    | Field | Required | Description | |---|---|---| | **Invoice Number** | Auto | Sequential
    number, auto-generated | | **Issue Date** | ✅ | When the invoice is dated | | **Due Date** | ✅
    | When payment is expected | | **Payment Terms** | Optional | Net 30, Net 60, Due on Receipt,
    etc. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Line Items">
    For each product or service: - **Description** — What you're billing for - **Quantity** — Number
    of units - **Unit Price** — Price per unit - **Amount** — Calculated automatically (Qty × Price)
    - **Category** — Revenue account to credit
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Notes">
    Include optional notes that appear on the invoice (e.g., payment instructions, thank you
    message).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">Click **Review** to preview the invoice as your customer will see it.</Step>
  <Step title="Send or Save">
    - **Save as Draft** — Keep editing later - **Send** — Email the invoice to the customer with a
    link to the payment portal
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invoice Preview

The review screen shows a professional-formatted invoice with:

- Your organization logo and details
- Customer billing information
- Itemized line items with subtotals
- Tax calculations (if applicable)
- Total amount due
- Payment terms and due date

## Journal Entry Generated

When an invoice is posted, Buwiz Books creates:

| Line | Account             | Debit     | Credit    |
| ---- | ------------------- | --------- | --------- |
| 1    | Accounts Receivable | $2,000.00 | —         |
| 2    | Revenue Account     | —         | $2,000.00 |

This records the revenue and tracks the amount owed by the customer.
