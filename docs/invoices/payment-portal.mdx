## Overview

The **Payment Portal** is a self-service web page where your customers can view their invoices and make payments. No login is required — each invoice has a unique, secure link.

## Customer Experience

When a customer receives an invoice email and clicks **"View & Pay"**:

1. They see the full invoice details — line items, amounts, due date
2. They can download a PDF copy
3. They can submit payment information
4. Upon payment, they receive a confirmation receipt

## Link Structure

Each invoice generates a unique payment URL:

```
https://your-domain.com/invoices/pay/{invoiceId}
```

This link is:

- **Unique per invoice** — cannot be guessed
- **No login required** — customers don't need an account
- **Read-only** — customers cannot edit the invoice

## Payment Recording

When a customer pays through the portal:

- The payment is automatically recorded against the invoice
- The invoice status updates to **Paid** (or **Partially Paid**)
- A journal entry is created:

| Line | Account             | Debit     | Credit    |
| ---- | ------------------- | --------- | --------- |
| 1    | Bank Account        | $2,000.00 | —         |
| 2    | Accounts Receivable | —         | $2,000.00 |

## Security

<Note>
  Payment portal links use cryptographic tokens. They cannot be enumerated or accessed without the
  specific link. The portal only shows the individual invoice — no access to other financial data.
</Note>
