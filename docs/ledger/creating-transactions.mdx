## Adding a New Transaction

Click the **+ Add Transaction** button in the top-right corner of the Ledger to open the transaction creation form.

## Transaction Types

The left sidebar of the transaction form lets you switch between four entry types. Each type has an optimized layout for its use case.

### Journal Entry

The default transaction type for general-purpose double-entry bookkeeping.

<img
  src="/images/ledger/add-transaction/journal-form.png"
  alt="Journal Entry form showing debit and credit lines with category, party, department, and location fields"
/>

**Key features:**

- Free-form debit and credit lines — you control exactly which accounts are affected
- Each line has its own **Category**, **Party**, **Department**, and **Location** selectors
- Add the **Memo** field for internal notes
- The bottom bar shows the running **Debit** and **Credit** totals — they must balance before saving

| Field                | Description                                   |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Date**             | The posting date (defaults to today)          |
| **Reference Number** | Optional reference for your records           |
| **Memo**             | A description of the transaction              |
| **Lines**            | Debit/credit entries with account assignments |

### Pay In

Use **Pay In** when your business receives money — customer payments, refunds received, or revenue deposits.

<img
  src="/images/ledger/add-transaction/pay-in-form.png"
  alt="Pay In form with party selector and deposit account fields"
/>

**Key features:**

- **Select Party** dropdown at the top to associate the payment with a customer or entity
- **Pay In Category** — the bank or cash account receiving the deposit
- **Receive For** lines — itemize what the payment covers (with Category, Department, Location)

### Pay Out

Use **Pay Out** to record money leaving the business — expenses, vendor payments, or refunds issued.

<img
  src="/images/ledger/add-transaction/pay-out-form.png"
  alt="Pay Out form showing expense category and multiple line items"
/>

**Key features:**

- **Select Party** dropdown to tag the vendor or payee
- **Pay Out Category** — the bank or cash account the payment comes from
- **Pay For** lines — itemize expenses with per-line Category, Department, and Location tagging
- Multiple line items let you split a single payment across different expense accounts

### Transfer

Use **Transfer** to move money between two accounts (e.g., bank-to-bank transfers).

<img
  src="/images/ledger/add-transaction/transfer-form.png"
  alt="Transfer form showing Transfer From and Transfer To with category and party selectors"
/>

**Key features:**

- **Transfer From** — the source account (Category + Party)
- **Transfer To** — the destination account (Category + Party)
- Clean two-panel layout with a visual **→** arrow between accounts
- Enter the dollar amount once — the system automatically balances the entry

## Document Attachments

Every transaction type supports document attachments via the right sidebar:

- **Browse or Drag & Drop** — upload images or PDFs (max 20MB)
- **Browse existing documents** — link to documents already in your document library
- Supported formats: Images (PNG, JPG, HEIC) and PDF

<Tip>
  Attach receipts and invoices directly to transactions for a complete audit trail. Auditors love
  this.
</Tip>

## Double-Entry Rules

<Note>
  Every transaction must balance — total debits must equal total credits. The form will not save if
  the entry is unbalanced. The balance indicator at the bottom shows ✅ green when balanced.
</Note>

### Example: Recording an Expense

To record a $500 payment for office supplies using **Pay Out**:

1. Click **+ Add Transaction** → select **Pay Out**
2. Set the Party to your vendor (e.g., "Staples")
3. Set **Pay Out Category** to your bank account
4. Add a line under **Pay For**: description "Office Supplies", amount $500
5. Set the Category to "Office Supplies (Expense)"
6. Click **Save & Close**

### Example: Recording Revenue

To record a $2,000 consulting invoice payment using **Pay In**:

1. Click **+ Add Transaction** → select **Pay In**
2. Set the Party to your customer (e.g., "Acme Corp")
3. Set **Pay In Category** to your bank account
4. Add a line under **Receive For**: description "Consulting services", amount $2,000
5. Set the Category to "Consulting Revenue"
6. Click **Save & Close**

## AI-Assisted Entry

When you start typing a description, Buwiz Books may suggest:

- **Matching categories** based on similar past transactions
- **Party auto-completion** from your entity database
- **Amount suggestions** from uploaded documents or receipts

## Saving Options

The **Save & Close** button (top-right, green) saves and returns to the ledger. Click the dropdown arrow **▾** next to it for additional options:

- **Save & New** — save and immediately open a fresh form
- **Save as Draft** — save without posting (if supported)

<Tip>
  You can also create transactions from within the Bill or Invoice workflows — those automatically
  generate the correct journal entries.
</Tip>
