## Step 1: Sign In

Navigate to your Buwiz Books instance and sign in with your credentials. Buwiz Books supports email/password authentication and Google OAuth.

<img src="/images/quickstart-login.png" alt="Login Screen" />

<Note>
  If this is your first time signing in, you'll be redirected to the onboarding flow to create your
  organization.
</Note>

## Step 2: Create Your Organization

After signing in for the first time, you'll be guided through organization setup:

1. **Organization Name** — Enter your business name
2. **Fiscal Year** — Select your fiscal year start month (default: January)
3. **Currency** — Choose your primary operating currency

<Tip>You can change these settings later from **Settings → Organization**.</Tip>

## Step 3: Set Up Your Chart of Accounts

Open the **Category Manager** (`/accounts`) and pick a starting template. Each one builds a complete
double-entry chart **and** fills in every category mapping, so bills and invoices post correctly from
your first transaction:

| Template                    | Accounts | Best for                                        |
| --------------------------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **General small business**  | 79       | Start here if none of the others fit            |
| **SaaS / startup**          | 87       | Subscription revenue, deferred revenue, hosting |
| **Freelancer / consultant** | 39       | Solo services — the leanest chart               |
| **Retail / e-commerce**     | 86       | Inventory, cost of goods, merchant fees         |

Before anything is written you get a **preview** showing exactly what will be created, what will be
reused, and which mappings will be filled. Applying a template never deletes or overwrites an
existing account — it matches on account number first, then on name, and only creates what is
genuinely missing. That makes it safe to run on a chart you have already started.

Every template produces the same 8 root account types:

| Account Type           | Code Range  | Purpose                             |
| ---------------------- | ----------- | ----------------------------------- |
| **Assets**             | 10000-19999 | Resources owned by the business     |
| **Liabilities**        | 20000-29999 | Debts owed to others                |
| **Equity**             | 30000-39999 | Owner's stake in the business       |
| **Revenue**            | 40000-49999 | Income from operations              |
| **Cost of Revenue**    | 50000-59999 | Direct costs of goods/services sold |
| **Operating Expenses** | 60000-69999 | Day-to-day business expenses        |
| **Other Income**       | 80000-89999 | Non-operating income                |
| **Other Expenses**     | 90000-99999 | Non-operating expenses              |

A template is a starting point, not a cage — add, rename, and deactivate accounts freely afterwards.
See [Managing Categories](/accounts/managing-categories) for editing the chart and
[Category Mappings](/admin/mappings) for the rules that route transactions into it.

## Step 4: Import Your Data

Buwiz Books supports importing data from other accounting systems:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Import Chart of Accounts">
    Go to **Settings → Import/Export** and upload a CSV file with your account names, numbers, and
    types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import Transactions">
    From the **Ledger**, click **⋯ → Import Transactions** to upload bank statements or transaction
    exports (CSV format).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import Entities">
    Vendors, customers, and other parties can be imported via CSV from **Settings → Import/Export**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
