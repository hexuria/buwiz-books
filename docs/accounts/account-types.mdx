## The Accounting Equation

All of Buwiz Books' account types derive from the fundamental accounting equation:

```
Assets = Liabilities + Equity + (Revenue − Expenses)
```

Each account type has a **normal balance** direction — either debit or credit. Understanding this determines how amounts appear in your reports.

## Account Type Reference

### Assets

<Info>Normal Balance: **Debit** | Code Range: **10000–19999**</Info>

Resources owned by the business that provide future economic benefit.

**Common sub-accounts:**

- Bank Accounts, Cash
- Accounts Receivable
- Prepaid Expenses
- Inventory
- Fixed Assets (equipment, vehicles, property)
- Investments

---

### Liabilities

<Info>Normal Balance: **Credit** | Code Range: **20000–29999**</Info>

Obligations the business owes to external parties.

**Common sub-accounts:**

- Accounts Payable
- Credit Cards
- Loans and Lines of Credit
- Accrued Expenses
- Payroll Liabilities
- Unearned Revenue

---

### Equity

<Info>Normal Balance: **Credit** | Code Range: **30000–39999**</Info>

The owner's residual interest after all liabilities are deducted from assets.

**Common sub-accounts:**

- Owner's Equity / Common Stock
- Retained Earnings
- Dividends / Distributions
- Owner's Draws

---

### Revenue

<Info>Normal Balance: **Credit** | Code Range: **40000–49999**</Info>

Income earned from the primary business operations.

**Common sub-accounts:**

- Sales Revenue
- Service Revenue
- Subscription Revenue
- Consulting Fees

---

### Cost of Revenue

<Info>Normal Balance: **Debit** | Code Range: **50000–59999**</Info>

Direct costs attributable to producing goods or delivering services sold.

**Common sub-accounts:**

- Cost of Goods Sold
- Direct Labor
- Materials and Supplies
- Shipping and Fulfillment

---

### Operating Expenses

<Info>Normal Balance: **Debit** | Code Range: **60000–69999**</Info>

Day-to-day costs of running the business, not directly tied to production.

**Common sub-accounts:**

- Rent and Utilities
- Salaries and Wages
- Marketing and Advertising
- Office Supplies
- Insurance
- Professional Fees

---

### Other Income

<Info>Normal Balance: **Credit** | Code Range: **80000–89999**</Info>

Income not related to primary operations.

**Common sub-accounts:**

- Interest Income
- Dividend Income
- Gain on Sale of Assets
- Foreign Exchange Gains

---

### Other Expenses

<Info>Normal Balance: **Debit** | Code Range: **90000–99999**</Info>

Costs not related to primary operations.

**Common sub-accounts:**

- Interest Expense
- Loss on Sale of Assets
- Foreign Exchange Losses
- Penalties and Fines
