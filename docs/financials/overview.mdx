## Overview

The **Financials** page provides five standard financial reports generated in real-time from your ledger data. Navigate to it from the sidebar.

## Available Reports

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Profit & Loss" icon="chart-line" href="/financials/profit-loss">
    Revenue minus expenses for a time period. Also known as the Income Statement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Balance Sheet" icon="scale-balanced" href="/financials/balance-sheet">
    Snapshot of assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cash Flow" icon="money-bill-wave" href="/financials/cash-flow">
    How cash moved through operating, investing, and financing activities.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Trial Balance" icon="list-check" href="/financials/trial-balance">
    All accounts with their debit and credit balances to verify the books balance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Aging Reports" icon="clock" href="/financials/aging-reports">
    Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable broken down by how overdue they are.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Navigation

Reports are organized as tabs across the top of the Financials page:

```
P&L | Balance Sheet | Cash Flow | Trial Balance | Aging
```

Click any tab to switch between reports. The selected date range persists across tabs.

## Date Controls

Financial reports use different date semantics depending on the report type:

| Report            | Date Meaning                                        |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **P&L**           | Revenue/expenses **during** the selected period     |
| **Balance Sheet** | Point-in-time snapshot **as of** the end date       |
| **Cash Flow**     | Cash movements **during** the selected period       |
| **Trial Balance** | Cumulative balances from inception through end date |
| **Aging**         | Outstanding amounts **as of** a specific date       |

## Comparison Mode

For Profit & Loss reports, you can enable comparison to see:

- **Prior Period** — Compare against the same-length prior period
- **Prior Year** — Compare against the same period in the previous year
- **Budget** — Compare against your budget (when configured)

Comparison shows both dollar and percentage change for each line item.

## Exporting Reports

All reports can be exported individually or as part of a [Financial Package](/ledger/financial-package):

- Click the **export** button on any report tab to download that report as XLSX
- Use the **Download Financial Package** from the Ledger to get all reports in a single ZIP
