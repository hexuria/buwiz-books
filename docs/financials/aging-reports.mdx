## What are Aging Reports?

**Aging Reports** break down outstanding amounts owed **to** your business (Accounts Receivable) and **by** your business (Accounts Payable) into time-based buckets showing how overdue each amount is.

<img src="/images/financials/aging-ar.png" alt="AR Aging Report" />

## Two Report Types

### Accounts Receivable (AR) Aging

Shows money **owed to you** by customers:

- Who owes you money?
- How old are the outstanding balances?
- Which customers are chronically late?

### Accounts Payable (AP) Aging

Shows money **you owe** to vendors:

- Who do you owe money to?
- How overdue are your payments?
- What bills need immediate attention?

Toggle between AR and AP using the **Aging Type** selector on the Aging tab.

## Age Buckets

Outstanding amounts are grouped into standard buckets:

| Bucket         | Age Range                  |
| -------------- | -------------------------- |
| **Current**    | Not yet due (0 days)       |
| **1–30 days**  | 1 to 30 days past due      |
| **31–60 days** | 31 to 60 days past due     |
| **61–90 days** | 61 to 90 days past due     |
| **90+ days**   | More than 90 days past due |

## Using Aging Reports

### Collections Priority

Focus on the **rightmost buckets** first:

- **90+ days** — High risk of becoming uncollectable. Escalate immediately
- **61–90 days** — Send payment reminders and follow up
- **31–60 days** — Standard follow-up process
- **Current/1–30** — Monitor, no action needed

### Vendor Payment Planning

For AP Aging:

- Pay items approaching **due date** to maintain vendor relationships
- Prioritize vendors with **early payment discounts**
- Flag **overdue** items for immediate payment

<Tip>
  Run Aging Reports weekly as part of your cash management routine. The trend over time is more
  important than any single snapshot.
</Tip>
