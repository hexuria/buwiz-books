## What is a Balance Sheet?

The **Balance Sheet** is a financial statement showing your business's **financial position at a specific point in time**. It follows the accounting equation:

```
Assets = Liabilities + Equity
```

## Key Concepts

| Concept           | Meaning                                                   |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Assets**        | Everything the business owns that has value               |
| **Liabilities**   | Everything the business owes to others                    |
| **Equity**        | The owner's residual interest (assets minus liabilities)  |
| **Balance Check** | Total Assets must always equal Total Liabilities + Equity |

<Note>
  The Balance Sheet is a **point-in-time** snapshot. It uses the **end date** of your selected date
  range, showing cumulative balances from inception through that date.
</Note>

## Reading the Balance Sheet

- **Increasing Assets** typically means the business is growing
- **High Liabilities relative to Assets** may indicate financial risk
- **Positive Equity** means the business is worth more than it owes
- **Assets = Liabilities + Equity** must always hold true — if it doesn't, there's a booking error
