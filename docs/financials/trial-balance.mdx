## What is a Trial Balance?

A **Trial Balance** lists every account in your Chart of Accounts with its total debit and credit balances. The sum of all debits must equal the sum of all credits — if they don't, there's an error in your books.

## Key Points

<Note>
  The Trial Balance shows **cumulative balances from inception** through the as-of date, not just
  the selected period. This is the standard accounting treatment.
</Note>

| Concept                        | Meaning                                             |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Debit Total = Credit Total** | Books are in balance ✅                             |
| **Debit ≠ Credit**             | There's a booking error that needs investigation ❌ |
| **Zero-balance accounts**      | Accounts with no activity are omitted by default    |

## Using the Trial Balance

The Trial Balance is primarily a **verification tool**:

1. **Month-end close** — Run after posting all entries to verify balance
2. **Before generating reports** — Ensures P&L and Balance Sheet will be accurate
3. **Error detection** — If totals don't match, investigate recent entries
4. **Auditor requests** — Often the first document requested in an audit

## Troubleshooting Unbalanced Entries

If your Trial Balance doesn't balance:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Recent Entries">
    Look at the most recently posted transactions for any one-sided entries
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Import Batches">
    CSV imports occasionally create unbalanced entries if column mapping was incorrect
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Opening Balances">
    Ensure opening balances were entered with proper debits and credits
  </Step>
</Steps>
