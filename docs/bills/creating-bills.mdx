## Creating a New Bill

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click + New Bill">From the Bills page, click the **+ New Bill** button.</Step>
  <Step title="Select Vendor">
    Choose the vendor from the dropdown or create a new vendor inline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Bill Details">
    | Field | Required | Description | |---|---|---| | **Vendor** | ✅ | Who the bill is from | |
    **Bill Number** | ✅ | The vendor's invoice number | | **Bill Date** | ✅ | When the bill was
    issued | | **Due Date** | ✅ | When payment is expected | | **Currency** | ✅ | Payment currency
    |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Line Items">
    Add one or more line items: - **Description** — What was purchased - **Category** — Account to
    charge (e.g., Office Supplies, Rent) - **Amount** — Dollar amount for this line - **Department**
    / **Location** — Optional dimensional tags
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach Documents">
    Upload the original vendor invoice (PDF or image) for record-keeping.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save as Draft** to save for later, or **Post** to finalize and create the corresponding
    journal entry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## AI-Assisted Bill Entry

Instead of manually entering details, you can:

1. Click **Scan Bill** or drag-and-drop a PDF/image
2. AI extracts: vendor name, bill number, date, due date, line items, and amounts
3. Review and confirm the extracted data
4. Save — all fields are pre-filled

See [AI Bill Scanning](/bills/ai-bill-scanning) for details.

## Journal Entry Generated

When a bill is posted, Buwiz Books automatically creates a journal entry:

| Line | Account                         | Debit   | Credit  |
| ---- | ------------------------------- | ------- | ------- |
| 1    | Expense Account (per line item) | $500.00 | —       |
| 2    | Accounts Payable                | —       | $500.00 |

This increases your liability (AP) and records the expense simultaneously.
