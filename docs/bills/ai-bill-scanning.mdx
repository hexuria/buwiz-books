## Overview

Buwiz Books uses AI-powered Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to extract data from uploaded bill documents, eliminating manual data entry.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload a Document">
    Drag and drop a bill PDF or image (JPG, PNG) onto the bill creation form, or click **Scan
    Bill**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI Processing">
    The AI engine processes the document and extracts: - **Vendor name** and contact details -
    **Bill number** / invoice number - **Bill date** and **due date** - **Line items** with
    descriptions and amounts - **Tax amounts** and totals - **Currency** detection
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Extracted Data">
    All extracted fields are pre-filled in the bill form. Review each field for accuracy: - Green
    indicators show high-confidence extractions - Amber indicators suggest fields that need
    verification
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct and Save">
    Fix any misread values, assign categories to line items, and save the bill.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supported Document Types

| Format      | Support Level | Notes                                                      |
| ----------- | ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **PDF**     | ✅ Best       | Highest accuracy, supports multi-page documents            |
| **JPG/PNG** | ✅ Good       | Photos of bills work, but ensure good lighting and clarity |
| **HEIC**    | ✅ Good       | iPhone photos are automatically converted                  |

## Accuracy Tips

<Tip>
  For best results, ensure the document is: - **Clear and readable** — avoid blurry or skewed images
  - **Complete** — include all pages of multi-page bills - **Standard format** — structured invoices
  extract better than handwritten notes
</Tip>

## Vendor Matching

When a bill is scanned, Buwiz Books automatically:

1. Looks up the extracted vendor name in your existing entities
2. If a match is found, the vendor is auto-linked
3. If no match, you can create a new vendor from the extracted details

## What Gets Created

After confirming the extracted data:

- A new **Bill** record with all details
- The original document is **attached** to the bill as supporting evidence
- Categories are **suggested** based on the vendor's history and description patterns
