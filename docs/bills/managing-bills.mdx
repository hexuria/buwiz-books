## Viewing Bill Details

Click any bill in the list to open its detail view showing:

- Full bill information (vendor, dates, amounts)
- Line items with category assignments
- Payment history
- Attached documents
- Comments and audit trail

## Recording Payments

To record a payment against a bill:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Bill">Click on the bill you want to pay.</Step>
  <Step title="Click Record Payment">Click the **Record Payment** button.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter Payment Details">
    - **Payment Date** — When the payment was made - **Amount** — Full or partial amount - **Payment
    Account** — Which bank/cash account the money came from - **Reference** — Check number, transfer
    reference, etc.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">The payment is recorded and the bill balance updates automatically.</Step>
</Steps>

The payment generates a journal entry:

| Line | Account          | Debit   | Credit  |
| ---- | ---------------- | ------- | ------- |
| 1    | Accounts Payable | $500.00 | —       |
| 2    | Bank Account     | —       | $500.00 |

## Partial Payments

Bills can be paid in installments:

- Each payment reduces the outstanding balance
- The bill status changes to **Partially Paid**
- When the balance reaches zero, status becomes **Paid**

## Overdue Management

Bills past their due date are automatically flagged as **Overdue**. Use the bill list filters to:

- **Sort by due date** — See what needs attention first
- **Filter by status** — View only overdue items
- **Filter by vendor** — See all outstanding amounts per vendor

<Tip>
  Run the [AP Aging Report](/financials/aging-reports) regularly to get a comprehensive view of all
  outstanding payables.
</Tip>

## Voiding a Bill

To void a posted bill:

1. Open the bill detail view
2. Click **⋯ menu → Void**
3. Confirm — this creates a reversing journal entry

<Warning>
  Voiding a bill with recorded payments will also reverse those payment entries. Ensure this is
  intentional.
</Warning>
