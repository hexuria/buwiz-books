## What is Buwiz Books?

Buwiz Books is a modern, full-featured accounting platform designed for businesses that need professional-grade financial management without the complexity of legacy software. Built with AI at its core, Buwiz Books automates tedious bookkeeping tasks while giving you complete control over your financial data.

## Key Features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="General Ledger" icon="book">
    Full double-entry bookkeeping with smart date filtering, grouped views by account, and real-time
    balance tracking.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Financial Reports" icon="chart-bar">
    Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, Trial Balance, and Aging reports — all generated
    instantly from your ledger data.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bank Reconciliation" icon="scale-balanced">
    Match imported bank statements against your ledger entries with AI-assisted suggestions and
    auto-matching.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bills & Invoices" icon="file-invoice-dollar">
    Complete accounts payable and receivable workflows with AI-powered document scanning and a
    customer payment portal.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Document Management" icon="folder-open">
    Upload receipts, statements, and contracts. AI automatically classifies documents and links them
    to transactions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Multi-Dimensional Tracking" icon="sitemap">
    Tag transactions with Departments and Locations for granular reporting across business segments.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who is Buwiz Books for?

- **Small to medium businesses** managing their own books
- **Accountants and bookkeepers** serving multiple clients
- **Finance teams** needing real-time financial visibility
- **Business owners** who want AI-assisted automation without sacrificing control
