## Overview

The monthly close process ensures your financial records are complete and accurate before generating reports. Follow this checklist each month.

**Time required:** 30–60 minutes (depending on transaction volume)

## Monthly Close Checklist

### Week 1: Gather and Enter (Days 1-5)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Collect All Receipts">
    Upload any remaining receipts and invoices to **Documents**. Use AI scanning to extract data
    automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Manual Transactions">
    Record any transactions not already in the ledger: - Cash transactions - Manual adjustments -
    Prepaid expense amortization - Depreciation entries
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Uncategorized Items">
    Go to the **Ledger** and filter for uncategorized transactions. Assign categories to all
    entries.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Week 2: Reconcile (Days 5-10)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download Bank Statements">
    Download statements from all bank accounts and credit cards for the month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Reconciliation Sessions">
    For each account, create a new [Reconciliation](/reconciliations/overview) session with the
    statement ending balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match Transactions">
    Work through auto-matches and manually match remaining items. Create entries for any bank fees
    or interest not yet recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Reconciliation">
    Ensure all reconciliations show a $0.00 difference and mark them as complete.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Week 3: Review and Report (Days 10-15)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run Trial Balance">
    Navigate to **Financials → Trial Balance**. Verify that total debits equal total credits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review P&L">
    Check the **Profit & Loss** statement. Look for: - Unusual or unexpected amounts - Missing
    revenue or expenses - Categories that seem too high or too low
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Balance Sheet">
    Check the **Balance Sheet**. Verify: - Bank balances match reconciled amounts - Accounts
    Receivable matches outstanding invoices - Accounts Payable matches unpaid bills
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate Financial Package">
    From the Ledger, download the **Financial Package** ZIP for the month. Share with your
    accountant or stakeholders.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Lock the Period

<Steps>
  <Step title="Lock the Month">
    Go to **Settings → Period Close** and lock the completed month. This prevents accidental changes
    to historical data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Only lock a period once you're confident all entries are correct. Unlocking requires admin access
  and leaves an audit trail.
</Warning>

## Tips for a Smooth Close

<Tip>
  - **Don't wait until month-end** to enter transactions — daily or weekly bookkeeping makes the
  close much faster - **Use mapping rules** to auto-categorize recurring transactions - **Set
  calendar reminders** for the close process so it doesn't slip - **Keep a close checklist** — this
  page works great as your template!
</Tip>
