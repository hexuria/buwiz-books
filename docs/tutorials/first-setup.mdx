## Introduction

This tutorial walks through the complete setup process for a new Buwiz Books organization — from first login to being ready for daily bookkeeping.

**Time required:** ~30 minutes

## Step 1: Create Your Organization (5 min)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign In">
    Navigate to your Buwiz Books instance and sign in with Google or email/password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name Your Organization">
    Enter your business name exactly as it should appear on financial reports and invoices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Basics">
    - Set your **fiscal year start month** (January for most businesses) - Select your **primary
    currency** - Upload your **business logo** (optional, can be done later)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Set Up Your Chart of Accounts (10 min)

Start from a template, then customize it for your business:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply a Template">
    Go to **Category Manager** and choose the template closest to your business — General small
    business, SaaS / startup, Freelancer / consultant, or Retail / e-commerce. Review the preview,
    then apply. This creates the chart **and** fills in every category mapping, so bills and
    invoices post correctly straight away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Accounts">
    Review what was created and deactivate any you don't need. (Accounts with transactions can't be
    deleted — deactivating keeps the audit trail intact.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Custom Accounts">
    Add accounts specific to your business: - **Revenue accounts** — One per revenue stream
    (Consulting, Product Sales, etc.) - **Expense accounts** — One per expense category (Rent,
    Utilities, Marketing, etc.) - **Bank accounts** — One per real bank/credit card account
  </Step>
  <Step title="Alternative: Import">
    If migrating from another system, import your existing chart of accounts via CSV. Go to
    **Settings → Import/Export → Import Chart of Accounts**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3: Add Your Entities (5 min)

Set up the parties you transact with:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Bank Accounts">
    Go to **Entities → Banks & Cards** and add each bank account and credit card. Connect each to
    the corresponding Category Manager account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Key Vendors">
    Go to **Entities → Vendors** and add your main suppliers (landlord, utilities, payroll provider,
    etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Key Customers">Go to **Entities → Customers** and add your main clients.</Step>
</Steps>

## Step 4: Import Historical Data (5 min)

If you have existing financial data:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from Old System">
    Export your transaction history as a CSV from QuickBooks, Xero, or your bank.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import Transactions">
    From the **Ledger**, click **⋯ → Import Transactions**. Upload the CSV and map the columns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Run a **Trial Balance** from the Financials page to ensure debits equal credits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 5: Set Up Automations (5 min)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Category Mappings">
    Go to **Mappings** and create rules for recurring transaction descriptions (e.g., "ADP Payroll"
    → Salaries).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Party Mappings">
    Map cryptic bank descriptions to proper vendor names (e.g., "AMZN MKTP" → Amazon).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## You're Ready! 🎉

Your Buwiz Books organization is now set up. Here's your daily routine:

| Frequency     | Task                                             |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Daily**     | Review and categorize new transactions           |
| **Weekly**    | Upload receipts and documents                    |
| **Monthly**   | Reconcile bank statements, run financial reports |
| **Quarterly** | Generate financial package for stakeholders      |
