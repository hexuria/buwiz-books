## Overview

The **Financial Package** is a ZIP archive containing all your key financial reports for a specific period. This tutorial shows you how to generate one for your accountant, auditor, or investors.

**Time required:** 2 minutes

## Step-by-Step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Ledger">
    Click **Ledger** in the sidebar. This is where the export function lives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Date Range">
    Use the **Smart Date Filter** at the top to select the reporting period:
    - Click on the date range display
    - Select a preset (e.g., "Last Quarter", "Last Year") or enter custom dates
    - The date range shown here determines what's included in the package
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Export Menu">
    Click the **⋯** (three dots) menu button in the top-right area of the Ledger.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Download Financial Package">
    Click **"Download Financial Package…"** from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Reports to Include">
    A dialog appears showing 5 available reports:

    | Report | Format | Recommended |
    |---|---|---|
    | ✅ Transaction List | CSV | Always include for audit trail |
    | ✅ Trial Balance | XLSX | Include to verify books balance |
    | ✅ Profit & Loss | XLSX | Essential — shows profitability |
    | ✅ Balance Sheet | XLSX | Essential — shows financial position |
    | ✅ Cash Flow Statement | XLSX | Important for investor reporting |

    Uncheck any reports you don't need, or leave all checked for a complete package.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Download ZIP">
    Click the green **Download ZIP** button. The server generates all reports and downloads a ZIP file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share with Your Accountant">
    Send the downloaded ZIP file to your accountant, auditor, or stakeholders via email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's in the Package

```
Financial-Package-Q1-2026.zip
├── transactions.csv          ← Detailed transaction list
├── trial-balance.xlsx        ← Account balance verification
├── profit-loss.xlsx          ← Income statement
├── balance-sheet.xlsx        ← Financial position snapshot
└── cash-flow.xlsx            ← Cash movement summary
```

## Common Scenarios

| Audience                      | Reports to Include              | Date Range                 |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **Monthly accountant review** | All 5 reports                   | Last Month                 |
| **Quarterly board meeting**   | P&L + Balance Sheet + Cash Flow | This Quarter               |
| **Tax filing preparation**    | All 5 reports                   | Last Year                  |
| **Investor update**           | P&L + Cash Flow                 | Year-to-Date               |
| **Audit preparation**         | All 5 reports                   | Custom (full audit period) |

<Tip>
  If your accountant prefers a specific format or additional data, you can also export individual
  reports or CSV files separately from the Ledger.
</Tip>
