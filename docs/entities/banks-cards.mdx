## Overview

The **Banks & Cards** section tracks your business's financial accounts — checking accounts, savings accounts, credit cards, and other financial instruments.

<img src="/images/entities/banks.png" alt="Banks list" />

## Adding a Bank Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Entities → Banks & Cards">
    Click **Banks & Cards** in the Entities section of the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click + New Bank">Click the add button to create a new bank entity.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details">
    - **Bank Name** — Financial institution name (e.g., "Mercury Bank") - **Account Type** —
    Checking, Savings, Credit Card, etc. - **Account Number** — Last 4 digits for identification -
    **Connected Category** — Link to the corresponding account in your Chart of Accounts
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bank Detail Panel

Click any bank to open its detail panel showing:

- **Account Information** — Name, type, connected category
- **Current Balance** — Real-time balance from your ledger
- **Recent Transactions** — Latest entries for this account
- **View Insights** — Navigate to the transaction analysis view for this entity

## Account Connections

Banks can be connected to their corresponding Chart of Accounts entry:

- A "Mercury Checking 1231" bank entity links to the "Mercury Checking 1231" asset account
- This connection enables the reconciliation workflow
- Transactions imported from the bank are matched against entries in the connected account

<Tip>
  Always ensure your bank entities have the correct category connection. This is essential for
  reconciliation to work properly.
</Tip>
