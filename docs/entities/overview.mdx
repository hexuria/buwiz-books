## What are Entities?

**Entities** are the people and organizations your business transacts with — vendors, customers, employees, banks, shareholders, lenders, and government agencies.

## Common Features

All entity types share:

- **Detail Panel** — View entity information, contact details, and transaction history
- **Transaction Insights** — See all ledger entries linked to this entity
- **Spend/Revenue Analysis** — Track total amounts over time
- **Document Linking** — Attach contracts, agreements, and correspondence
- **Notes** — Internal comments and observations

## Entity Linking

Entities are linked to transactions, bills, and invoices throughout Buwiz Books:

- When you create a **bill**, the vendor entity is linked
- When you send an **invoice**, the customer entity is linked
- When you record a **payroll** entry, employee entities can be tagged
- **Bank accounts** are linked to their corresponding entities
