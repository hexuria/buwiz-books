## Overview

**Lenders** represent financial institutions, businesses, or individuals that have extended credit or loans to your organization.

<img src="/images/entities/lenders.png" alt="Lenders list" />

## Managing Lenders

### Creating a Lender

1. Navigate to **Entities → Lenders**
2. Click **+ New Lender**
3. Enter: institution name, loan details, contact information

### Lender Detail Panel

- **Loan Information** — Principal, interest rate, terms
- **Payment History** — All loan payments made
- **Outstanding Balance** — Remaining amount owed
- **Interest Expense** — Total interest paid over time
