## Overview

**Customers** are the businesses and individuals who purchase from you. Every invoice and revenue entry can be linked to a customer.

<img src="/images/entities/customers.png" alt="Customers list" />

## Managing Customers

### Creating a Customer

1. Navigate to **Entities → Customers**
2. Click **+ New Customer**
3. Enter customer details:
   - **Name** — Business or individual name
   - **Email** — For sending invoices
   - **Phone** — Contact number
   - **Billing Address** — For invoice generation

### Customer Detail Panel

Click any customer to see:

- **Contact Information** — Full details
- **Total Revenue** — Cumulative income from this customer
- **Invoice History** — All invoices sent to this customer
- **Outstanding Balance** — Unpaid invoice amounts
- **Transaction History** — All ledger entries linked to this customer

## Revenue Insights

Track revenue per customer:

- Total revenue over time
- Invoice payment patterns
- Average days to pay
- Outstanding receivables
