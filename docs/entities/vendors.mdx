## Overview

**Vendors** are the businesses and individuals you purchase goods and services from. Every bill and expense can be linked to a vendor for spending analysis.

<img src="/images/entities/vendors.png" alt="Vendors list" />

## Managing Vendors

### Creating a Vendor

1. Navigate to **Entities → Vendors**
2. Click **+ New Vendor**
3. Enter vendor details:
   - **Name** — Business or individual name
   - **Email** — Contact email
   - **Phone** — Contact number
   - **Address** — Mailing address
   - **Tax ID** — For 1099 reporting (optional)

### Vendor Detail Panel

Click any vendor to see:

- **Contact Information** — Full details
- **Total Spend** — Cumulative amount spent with this vendor
- **Transaction History** — All ledger entries linked to this vendor
- **Open Bills** — Outstanding unpaid bills
- **Documents** — Attached contracts or correspondence

## Spend Insights

The vendor detail view provides at-a-glance spending insights:

- Total spend over time
- Category breakdown (which accounts are most charged)
- Month-over-month trends
- Outstanding balance

<Tip>
  Click **View Insights** on any vendor to see a dedicated analysis page with charts and detailed
  transaction history.
</Tip>
