## Overview

**Shareholders** represent owners, investors, or partners who hold equity in your business. Track contributions, distributions, and ownership changes.

<img src="/images/entities/shareholders.png" alt="Shareholders list" />

## Managing Shareholders

### Creating a Shareholder

1. Navigate to **Entities → Shareholders**
2. Click **+ New Shareholder**
3. Enter: name, ownership percentage, contact details

### Shareholder Detail Panel

- **Ownership Details** — Percentage and share information
- **Contributions** — Capital invested in the business
- **Distributions** — Dividends and draws paid out
- **Transaction History** — All equity-related entries
