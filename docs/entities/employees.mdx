## Overview

**Employees** represent your team members. Link payroll, reimbursement, and expense transactions to specific employees for tracking.

<img src="/images/entities/employees.png" alt="Employees list" />

## Managing Employees

### Creating an Employee

1. Navigate to **Entities → Employees**
2. Click **+ New Employee**
3. Enter: name, email, role, department

### Employee Detail Panel

- **Personal Information** — Name, contact, department
- **Payroll History** — Salary and wage payments
- **Reimbursements** — Expense reimbursement transactions
- **Total Compensation** — Cumulative payments over time
