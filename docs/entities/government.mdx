## Overview

**Government** entities represent tax authorities, licensing agencies, or other regulatory bodies your business interacts with.

<img src="/images/entities/government.png" alt="Government entities list" />

## Managing Government Entities

### Common Government Entities

- **IRS** — Federal income tax, payroll tax
- **State Tax Authority** — State income tax, sales tax
- **Local Tax Office** — City/county taxes, permits
- **Social Security Administration** — FICA payments

### Government Detail Panel

- **Agency Information** — Name, account/ID numbers
- **Tax Payments** — History of all tax remittances
- **Filing Deadlines** — Upcoming due dates
- **Total Paid** — Cumulative tax payments
