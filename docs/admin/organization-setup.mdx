## Creating an Organization

<img src="/images/admin/organization-setup.png" alt="Organization Setup Onboarding" />

When you first sign in to Buwiz Books, you'll be guided through the onboarding flow:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Organization Name">
    Enter your business name. This appears throughout the application and on invoices/reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Business Details">
    Configure basic business information: - **Fiscal Year Start** — The month your fiscal year
    begins (default: January) - **Currency** — Primary operating currency (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.) -
    **Industry** — Optional industry classification
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chart of Accounts">
    Buwiz Books creates a standard chart of accounts with 8 root types. You can customize this
    later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Team Members">Optionally invite colleagues to join the organization.</Step>
</Steps>

## Multi-Organization Support

Buwiz Books supports multiple organizations per user:

- Each organization is completely isolated — separate accounts, transactions, entities, and settings
- Use the **Organization Switcher** in the sidebar header to switch between organizations
- You can be a member of multiple organizations with different roles in each

## Joining an Organization

If you've been invited to an existing organization:

1. Sign in with your credentials
2. Navigate to `/organizations/join`
3. Enter the invitation code or accept the email invitation
4. You're automatically added to the organization

<Note>
  Some organizations operate in **Invite-Only** mode, meaning new users must be explicitly invited
  by an admin. If you're removed from all organizations in invite-only mode, you'll be signed out.
</Note>
