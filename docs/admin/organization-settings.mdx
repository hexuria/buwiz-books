## Accessing Settings

Navigate to **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar. The settings page is organized into sections:

## Review Rules

The checks every Inbox entry goes through (on or off, **Stop** or **Warn**, thresholds), the
**Scan books** ledger scan, rule snapshots, and the answers the Inbox remembers (**Memories**).
The old Review Agents page now opens here.

## Jev Approval

Jev can approve Inbox papers on its own, but only once it has earned it, and only one **lane** at
a time — one vendor or customer and one kind of paper (say, _Acme Hosting · Vendor bill_). Admins
make the changes here; everyone can see the lanes.

| Setting                          | What it does                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Let Jev approve Inbox papers** | The organization's switch, **off** by default. Off, no lane approves anything; lanes keep learning                             |
| **Maker-checker opt-in**         | Shown when the organization requires a different approver. Unticked, every approval stays with a person                        |
| **Spot checks**                  | The share of papers Jev would approve that is left in the Inbox for a person instead (10% by default)                          |
| **Lanes**                        | Each lane's level, how often people agreed with Jev, how many reviews that rests on, its calibration, and its limits once auto |

**Levels.** A lane starts at **Watch** the first time Jev answers one of its papers: Jev's answers
are compared with what people decide, and nothing else changes. At **Suggest**, the Inbox says
when Jev would approve a paper; a person still approves it. At **Auto**, Jev approves papers that
pass every check, except the spot-check share. An admin promotes a lane one step at a time, and
only after 200 of Jev's own answers on that lane were reviewed with at least 98% accepted
unchanged; Auto also needs an **amount cap** and a **confidence threshold** the lane's calibration
supports. If agreement over a lane's last 50 papers falls below 95%, it drops back to Suggest by
itself. Admins can demote a lane at any time.

**Always a person.** Whatever a lane's level, these always wait in the Inbox: a new vendor or
customer, a paper asking to pay different bank details, an emailed paper whose sender could not be
verified (the Inbox says **Sender could not be verified**), a possible duplicate, a closed period,
an open check or warning, anything over the lane's cap or below its threshold, and every spot
check. An emailed paper counts as verified only when the email passed sender authentication and
came from an address or domain this vendor or customer already uses.

**Spot checks** show in the Inbox as **Spot check**: Jev would have approved the paper, and your
decision is an honest check on its work.

**The AI kill switch** (under **AI Credentials**) stops every AI feature at once, Jev approval
included.

**Undo.** Entries Jev approved carry a **by Jev** tag in Bills and Transactions. On the entry,
**Undo Jev approval** (you need permission to approve Inbox items; a note of what was wrong is
optional) reverses it — the
entry is never deleted: a reversal is posted, a bill Jev created is voided if nothing was paid on
it, and the paper returns to the Inbox. The undo counts against the lane.

## Period Close

Lock accounting periods to prevent changes to historical data:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Period Close">Go to **Settings → Period Close**.</Step>
  <Step title="Select Period">Choose the month and year to lock.</Step>
  <Step title="Lock Period">
    Click **Lock**. All transactions within this period become read-only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Locked periods cannot be edited by any user. Only admins can unlock periods, and doing so should
  be accompanied by a clear audit reason.
</Warning>
