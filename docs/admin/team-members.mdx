## Inviting Members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Settings">Go to **Settings** → **Team Members**.</Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite">Click **+ Invite Member**.</Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details">
    - **Email** — The member's email address - **Role** — Assign a role (Admin, Member, Viewer)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Invitation">
    The member receives an email with a link to join your organization.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Roles and Permissions

| Role       | Permissions                                                       |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Admin**  | Full access — manage settings, members, lock periods, delete data |
| **Member** | Create and edit transactions, bills, invoices, and documents      |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to all data — cannot create or modify            |

## Managing Members

From the Team Members section you can:

- **Change roles** — Promote or demote members
- **Remove members** — Revoke access to the organization
- **View activity** — See when each member last signed in

<Note>
  Removing a member does not delete their past activity. All transactions they created remain in the
  ledger with their name in the audit trail.
</Note>
