## Accessing Your Profile

Click your **name/avatar** at the bottom of the sidebar to open Profile Settings.

<img src="/images/admin/profile-settings.png" alt="Profile settings" />

## Personal Information

| Field            | Description                              |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Display Name** | Your name as shown throughout the app    |
| **Email**        | Your login email address                 |
| **Avatar**       | Profile picture (upload or use Gravatar) |

## Security

### Changing Password

1. Navigate to **Profile → Security**
2. Enter your current password
3. Enter and confirm your new password
4. Click **Update Password**

### Two-Factor Authentication

Enable 2FA for additional account security:

1. Navigate to **Profile → Security**
2. Click **Enable 2FA**
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app
4. Enter the verification code to confirm

## Active Sessions

View all devices where you're currently signed in:

- **Device** — Browser and OS information
- **Location** — Approximate location
- **Last Active** — When the session was last used
- **Revoke** — Sign out from specific devices

## Sign Out

Click **Sign Out** at the bottom of the sidebar to end your session on this device.
